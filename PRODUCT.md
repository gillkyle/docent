# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Docent is for Samsung Frame TV owners who want to manage their own art without wrestling with Samsung's built-in interface. The primary user is non-technical: comfortable using a browser, but not interested in APIs, network details, or media pipeline concepts.

## Product Purpose

Docent gives Frame TV owners a local, private art manager: upload artwork, organize it into collections, choose what appears on the TV, and eventually schedule collections by time and day. Success means the user can treat the Frame like a calm household art display, not a smart-TV settings chore.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, capable, and curated. The product should feel like a respectful collection manager: calm enough for living-room artwork, clear enough for household use, and confident enough to hide technical machinery until it is needed.

## Anti-references

Avoid Samsung's cluttered built-in TV UI, overly decorative gallery websites, generic SaaS-dashboard blandness, and home-automation panels that expose too many raw controls. The interface should not feel theatrical, gamified, or configuration-heavy.

## Design Principles

- Put artwork first, with controls close at hand but visually secondary.
- Make common household tasks obvious: upload, organize, display, schedule.
- Hide network and TV implementation details behind plain-language status and recovery.
- Prefer quiet density over decoration: useful information, restrained surfaces, minimal ceremony.
- Design for trust: every action should make it clear what will happen to the TV.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible controls. Support reduced-motion preferences, readable labels, and clear non-color status indicators for non-technical users managing the app from laptops, tablets, or phones.
